# CargoTribe: demo video scripts and shot lists

Three short screen recordings for the landing page and for social posts. Record on a phone-sized
screen (390 x 844) from a local or staging copy with demo data, never from production. Each is
15 to 30 seconds, with captions on screen and no voice needed. Spoken versions are optional.

Rules for every video:
- Claim only what the product does. No savings figures or customer quotes until real ones exist.
- Show test companies only. No real company names, prices or people.
- Dates DD/MM/YYYY, money in AUD with a dollar sign.
- Add captions in English first. A Simplified Chinese caption set follows once the dictionary is written
  and a native speaker has reviewed it.

---

## 1. "Post a job in 30 seconds" (for overseas agents)

Caption on screen: **From cargo to quotes in about 30 seconds**

| Shot | What happens on screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (3s) | Sign-in page, email typed, "Email me a sign-in link" tapped | Sign in with your email. No password. |
| 2 (3s) | First screen: tap "I need a quote" | Choose what you need today |
| 3 (10s) | Request form: Shenzhen to Melbourne, FCL, 2 x 40HC, "Flat-pack furniture", FOB. The scope strip lights up | Route, cargo, terms. That is all |
| 4 (3s) | Tap "Post request"; the job page opens, "Open for quotes" | Posted. Forwarders are watching this lane |
| 5 (4s) | "Repeat last job" button on the request page, tapped | Shipping it again next month? One tap |
| End (2s) | CargoTribe logo and cargotribe.com.au | Join the tribe |

## 2. "Compare quotes like for like" (agents, the hero feature)

Caption on screen: **Every quote, laid out the same way**

| Shot | What happens on screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (3s) | Job page, "Quotes (2)" heading | Two forwarders have quoted |
| 2 (6s) | Scroll the two quote cards: coverage chips (Destination port, Customs, Delivery) tick on one, a red cross on Delivery on the other | See what each quote covers |
| 3 (4s) | Warning line "Does not include: Delivery" | Missing legs are flagged for you |
| 4 (5s) | Tap "Show only quotes that cover everything" | Hide the incomplete ones |
| 5 (4s) | Tap "Award to ...", confirm | Award the best quote |
| 6 (4s) | Contact details appear on the job | Contact details unlock for you and the winner only |

Note: record this with quotes whose lines are tagged (the "Covers" setting), so the chips show.

## 3. "Quote in seconds from your rate card" (for forwarders)

Caption on screen: **Set your prices once. Quote in seconds.**

| Shot | What happens on screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (3s) | My lanes, "Upload a sheet" or "New rate card" | Save your port charges and cartage once |
| 2 (5s) | A saved rate card: Port Botany, three charges | One card per port |
| 3 (3s) | Email alert on the phone: "New job on your lane" | Get an email the moment a matching job is posted |
| 4 (8s) | Open the job: the quote is already filled in, "Check every line, then send" banner | The quote opens already filled in |
| 5 (4s) | Tap "Send sealed quote" | Your price stays sealed. Only the requester sees it |
| End (2s) | Logo and cargotribe.com.au | Join the tribe |
